# Sharpflow ClickUp Health — Lead-Magnet Playbook

A short guide for using the ClickUp Health check to turn prospects into booked calls.
Everything here is copy-paste; swap the `[bracketed]` bits for the prospect's details.

---

## What it is

A free, branded web app: a prospect connects their ClickUp (read-only) and gets an instant
**0–100 health score**, category breakdown, prioritised findings and a downloadable PDF.
It ends with **"Book a call with Sharpflow"**.

Link to hand out: **https://health.sharpflowconsulting.com**
Sample report (share this first): **https://health.sharpflowconsulting.com/sample**

---

## The flow

1. **Attract** — share the *sample report* link (no connection needed) in outreach, LinkedIn or DMs.
2. **Connect** — the prospect clicks *Connect ClickUp*, authorises read-only, picks their workspace.
3. **Report** — they instantly see their score, findings and PDF.
4. **Capture** — they land in your **Sharpflow ClickUp CRM** (Sales Pipeline) as a lead.
5. **Follow up** — use the templates below; the goal is always the same: a 30-min review call.

> The report does the selling. Your job is to follow up while the score is fresh (same day is best).

---

## Where to share it

- **Cold outreach** — lead with the sample, offer to run it on *their* workspace.
- **LinkedIn** — post the sample with a "most ClickUp workspaces score under 70 — here's why" hook.
- **Existing clients** — run it as a free health check; great for retention and upsells.
- **Proposals** — attach their report as evidence for the work you're pitching.

## Tips

- **Lead with the number.** "[Company] scored 61/100" is a stronger opener than "we do ClickUp consulting".
- **Personalise the follow-up** with their real findings and workspace name (pull it from the lead in the CRM).
- **Same-day follow-up** converts best — the score is front-of-mind.
- **Keep the sample handy.** It lets people evaluate you before they connect anything.
- **Every report ends in a lead** in your Sales Pipeline — work that list weekly.
